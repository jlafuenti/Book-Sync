## Upload only content you are entitled to hold

You may put a file on this server only if you are legally entitled to hold a copy of it. That
covers books you bought, books you borrowed under a licence that lets you keep a local copy, and
material that is in the public domain or that you wrote yourself.

This applies in full to the DRM-related import paths — the ACSM (Adobe) and Audible pipelines
described in [import-sources.md](import-sources.md). Those exist so that **you** can read **your
own** purchases on **your own** hardware. Do not use them, or this server, on files you do not
have the right to hold, and do not redistribute anything you upload here to anyone else.

The operator can remove any file, at any time, without asking first.
